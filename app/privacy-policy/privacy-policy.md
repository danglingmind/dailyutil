## Information We Collect

### Permissions Used
Our app requires the following permissions:

1. **Camera Permission**: We request access to your device's camera to allow you to take photos for profile pictures and other features within the app. We do not capture photos without your explicit action.

2. **Location Permission**: We request access to your device's location to show your position on maps and provide location-based services. Location data is only accessed when you use the app and is not tracked in the background.

3. **Internet Access**: Required for the app to communicate with our servers and provide its core functionality.

4. **Storage Permission**: Required to save images you choose to upload or download through our app.
